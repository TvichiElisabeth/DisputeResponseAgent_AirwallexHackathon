# Dispute Desk

## Design
Visual style: @docs/design.md
Design mockups live in design/*.dc.html — reference only, rebuild with our components.

How to apply style.md to the app (it is written for marketing pages):
- Use: color tokens, Inter as the font (Suisse Intl is not licensed),
  regular-weight headings with tight tracking, hairline rules instead of shadows,
  3.6px corners, cyan/blue edge light, motion timings and easings.
- Do not use in app screens: the hero constellation, textured scenes,
  the dark footer, 9px/11.7px marketing text sizes.
  App body text stays 14px, labels 12px, buttons 44px tall for touch targets.
- Urgent red #C4262B is allowed for deadlines and "hurts us"; it is not in the guide.
- Status markers are the 6×12 half-D shape from the logo
  (solid = decided, outline = needs you), never pill badges.
